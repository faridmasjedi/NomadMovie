Download & install

Version latest . Pick your system. Your system is highlighted.

⬇ Open the latest release on GitHub

macOS

  1. Download the .dmg for your Mac: Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) or Intel. To check, open Apple menu → About This Mac.
  2. Open the .dmg and drag NomadMovie into Applications.
  3. The first time, right-click NomadMovie in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. macOS asks this once because the app isn't from the App Store. If you only see Move to Trash, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Windows

  1. Download and run NomadMovie-…-windows-setup.exe.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. No installer wanted? Download the .zip, unzip it anywhere and run NomadMovie.exe.

Linux

  1. AppImage (any distribution): make it executable (chmod +x NomadMovie-*.AppImage) and run it. Some distributions need libfuse2.
  2. Debian / Ubuntu: sudo apt install ./NomadMovie-*.deb.
What's inside. The app includes everything it needs: the editor, its engine, ffmpeg for converting and exporting, and the fonts. AI features are optional; see Connect an AI.

Updates

The app tells you when a new version is out. On Windows (installer) and Linux (AppImage) it installs it for you when you restart. On macOS, click Download in the notice and install the new version over the old one; your projects stay.

  • Corner handle: resize. Side handles: stretch. Top handle: rotate.

Inspector

Timeline

  • Per track: show/hide, mute, lock, delete
  • Toolbar: undo, redo, split, delete, ripple delete, duplicate, marker, snapping, add tracks, zoom
  • Drag the divider above the timeline to make it taller.

Get started

Your first video in 5 minutes

A short travel clip with music, a title, a transition and a color look, exported as MP4.

  1. Import. In Media, drop your clips, photos and a music file onto the import box, or click it to browse. Any format works. See Import.
  2. Add to the timeline. Double-click each clip (or drag it to a track). Clips line up one after another. Music goes onto an audio track.
  3. Trim. Drag the left or right edge of a clip to shorten it. Move the playhead and press S to split, then delete the part you don't want with Shift+Delete to close the gap.
  4. Transition. Open Transitions and drag Cross Dissolve (or anything you like) onto the cut between two clips.
  5. Title. Open Text, click a style, type your title in the Inspector and pick an In animation on the Animation tab.
  6. Look. Select a clip, open the Color tab and click a look such as Cinematic. Save it as a preset to reuse it on the other clips.
  7. Music. Drag the fade handles on the music clip to fade it in and out. If people speak, right-click the music → Smart audio → Auto-duck under speech.
  8. Export. Click Export, keep MP4 and High, and click Render. Choose where to save it.
Shortcut. With an AI connected, steps 4–7 can be one sentence in the AI tab: “add cross dissolves between the clips, a title ‘Lisbon 2026’ at the start, a cinematic look and fade the music in and out.”

Import any video, audio or photo

Drop files on the import box in Media or click it to browse. Files the editor can't play directly are converted for you, with progress shown at the top.

Supported formats

TypeFormats
VideoMP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WMV, FLV, MPEG/MPG, MTS/M2TS/TS (camcorders), WebM, 3GP, MXF, OGV, VOB, DV, RM/RMVB, DivX/XviD. Codecs: H.264, HEVC/H.265, ProRes, DNxHD, AV1, VP9, MPEG-2/4, VC-1.
AudioMP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC/M4A, OGG, OPUS, WMA, AIFF, AC-3, ALAC, APE, AMR, CAF, MKA
PhotosJPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, SVG, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF (iPhone), RAW camera files (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, ORF, RW2, RAF…), PSD, TIFF, TGA, EXR
AnimationLottie (.json), see Lottie animations

How conversion works

  • Plays directly (MP4, MOV and WebM with common codecs, most audio and photos): used as is.
  • Repackaged (for example MKV, MTS or MOV with H.264 or HEVC): the video is moved into an MP4 without re-encoding. No quality loss, about half a second for a short clip.
  • Converted (AVI, WMV, ProRes, MPEG-2…): re-encoded to high-quality H.264 (CRF 17). Sound becomes AAC; audio-only files become lossless FLAC.
  • Photos like HEIC, RAW and PSD are converted to JPG, or PNG when they have transparency.
Your originals are never changed. The converted copy is stored inside the project.

Using your library

  • Double-click a clip or click its + to add it to the end of the timeline. Audio goes in at the playhead.
  • Drag a clip to a track and position.
  • Filter with All / Video / Audio / Image. × deletes a file and its clips.
  • Import photos opens a picker that shows only images. Photo slideshow puts all photos on the timeline (see Photo slideshow).
Limits. Only the first audio track of multi-language videos is used, and embedded subtitles aren't imported. Very large files (several GB) use a lot of memory because their sound is analysed for the waveform.

Timeline editing

Arrange clips on as many video and audio tracks as you need. Every change can be undone.

Tracks

  • + Video track and + Audio track add tracks. Rename a track by clicking its name.
  • Upper video tracks are drawn in front of lower ones, so put titles and stickers on top.
  • Each track can be hidden 👁, muted 🔊, locked 🔒 (no edits) or deleted ×.
  • Clips show their effects as small chips and their transitions as badges on the cuts. Click either to edit it.

Speed and duration

Select a video or audio clip and open the Speed tab: 0.1× to 8×, with quick buttons from ×0.25 to ×8. The clip gets shorter or longer to match. Use 0.5× or less for slow motion.

Shapes, colors and gradients

The Elements panel adds shapes, solid color backgrounds and full-frame gradients.

  • Shapes: rectangle, circle, triangle, star, heart, arrow, line. They pop in and fade out by default.
  • Shape settings: type, width, height, fill color, a second color for a gradient, corner radius (rectangles), stroke width and color.

90 GPU effects in 9 categories. Hover a tile to see it move, then drag it onto a clip.

Adding and adjusting effects

  1. Open Effects. Search or pick a category.
  2. Drag an effect onto a clip, or select clips and click the effect.
  3. The Inspector opens the clip's Effects tab. Every effect has an Amount and, depending on the effect, settings such as radius, speed, angle, center or colors.
  4. Effects stack from top to bottom: use ↑ to reorder, the switch to turn one off, × to remove it.

Effects are listed as chips on the clip in the timeline; click a chip to jump to its settings. Dropping an effect on an empty part of a track creates an effect layer instead.

Create

Effect layers

An effect layer applies its effects and color settings to everything on the tracks below it, for as long as it lasts, like an adjustment layer in After Effects.

  1. Drag an effect or a look onto an empty spot of a track above your clips. Or click an effect with nothing selected, or use + Empty effect layer.
  2. Drag its edges to cover the part of the video you want.
  3. Add more effects and color settings to it like any clip.
  4. To limit it to one area, such as blurring a face, give the layer a mask. Invert affects everything outside the mask.

Create

Green screen (chroma key)

Remove a background color so the layers below show through.

  1. Put the background clip on a lower track and the green-screen clip above it.
  2. Select the green-screen clip. In the Basic tab, open Chroma key (green screen) and turn on Enabled.
  3. Set Key color to the background color (green by default; blue works too).
  4. Raise Threshold until the background disappears; use Softness for smoother edges.
No green screen? Use AI cut-out to separate a person from any background.

Masks

Keep only part of a picture, or remove a part, by drawing on the preview.

  1. Select a clip and open Mask & cut-out in the Basic tab.
  2. Choose a tool and draw on the preview: ▭ Box or ◯ Ellipse (drag), ✎ Freehand (draw the outline), ⬠ Points (click point by point).
  3. Adjust with ✏ Edit outline: drag the points.
SettingWhat it does
EnabledTurns the mask on or off without deleting it.
InvertRemoves the inside instead of keeping it.
Curved edgesSmooths the outline between points.
FeatherSoftens the edge, 0 to 200 px.
ExpandGrows or shrinks the outline, −100 to 100 px.
Animate ◆Adds a mask key at the playhead. With keys, editing the outline sets a key and the mask moves smoothly between keys. ‹ › jump between keys.
⛶ Crop to maskCuts the frame down to the box around the mask.

On an effect layer, the mask limits the effect to an area: blur a face, brighten the sky, or with Invert, darken everything else.

Create

Cut out a person with one click

Click a person or object and it's outlined for you. Track follows it through the clip. It runs on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Select a video or photo clip. In Mask & cut-out, click ✨ Select a subject with AI.
  2. Click the person or object in the preview. A mask appears around it. Click again elsewhere to refine.
  3. For videos, choose how often to look (Every frame to Every 5th frame) and click Track forward ▶ (or ◀ Track back). ■ Stop stops any time.
  4. Use Feather and Expand for a cleaner edge, or Invert to remove the subject and keep the background.
Under the hood. NomadMovie uses EdgeTAM, a fast version of Meta's Segment Anything 2. The model (about 40 MB) downloads once from Hugging Face the first time you use it. It runs on your GPU with WebGPU, or on the processor otherwise. Expect about one second per frame on a 2019 laptop.
Make it pop. Add Outer Glow (Neon Cut-out), Outline / Stroke or Drop Shadow from Effects → Stylize to the cut-out clip.
Animate

Keyframes

Animate anything over time: position, scale, rotation, opacity, blur, stretch and volume.

  1. Select a clip and move the playhead to where the motion should start.
  2. Click ◆ next to a property (X, Y, Scale, Rotation, Opacity in Basic; Blur, Scale X, Scale Y in Animation → Keyframes).
  3. Move the playhead and change the value: with the slider, or by dragging the layer on the preview. Once a property has keyframes, every change makes a new keyframe at the playhead.
  4. Fine-tune in Animation → Keyframes: click a time to jump there, type an exact value, choose the easing, delete with ×, or Clear all.

Easing curves

LinearEase inEase outSmoothBackBounceElasticHold

Keyframes show as small diamonds on the clip in the timeline. The ◆ button is filled when there's a keyframe at the playhead, and outlined when the property is animated elsewhere.

Animate

Animate the parts of an illustration

Turn a flat SVG drawing into a puppet: split it into parts, choose what each part turns around, and link the parts together, like in After Effects.

  1. Import an .svg illustration and add it to the timeline.
  2. In the Basic tab, click ✂ Split into layers. Each top-level part (group) goes on its own track, named after its id or Inkscape/Illustrator layer name.
  3. Click a part on the preview to select it. Under Anchor point, pick one of the 9 positions or click 🎯 Pick and click the exact point it should turn around: a shoulder, a stem, a hinge.
  4. Under Parent, choose the part it should follow (hand → arm → body). It then moves, turns and scales with its parent.
  5. Animate with motion presets or keyframes.
Note. After splitting, all parts follow the bottom part so that moving it moves the whole drawing. Change any part's Parent to build your own hierarchy.
With AI. “Split the character into layers and make the arm wave.”
Animate

Lottie animations

Lottie is the format After Effects animations are exported to with Bodymovin, and what LottieFiles offers by the thousand. Drop a .json file in and it plays on the timeline.

  • Lottie clips loop, so make them as long as you like.
  • The Speed tab changes their speed. Position, size, effects and masks work like any layer.
  • Preview and export draw exactly the same frames.
Safe playback. Expressions (JavaScript inside a Lottie file) are ignored, so a file can't run code. Most animations don't use them.
Animate

Zoom and pan inside a clip

Camera moves without a camera: zoom into exactly the point you choose, punch in for emphasis, or pan across a photo.

  1. Select a clip, open Animation → Zoom & pan.
  2. Choose a Move: Zoom in, Zoom out, Punch in & back, Ken Burns, Pan left, right, up or down.
  3. Click 🎯 Pick on preview and click the point to zoom into. It stays fixed while everything else grows around it.
  4. Set Zoom (1.05× to 4×), where it starts (playhead or clip start), the duration or until the end of the clip, and the easing.
  5. Click Apply move. It creates keyframes you can fine-tune. Clear zoom & pan removes them.

Faster: right-click a clip → Zoom & pan → Zoom in here, Punch in & back or Ken Burns.

Record a voice-over

Narrate while you watch, straight onto the timeline.

  1. Move the playhead to where the narration should start.
  2. Open the Audio panel and click ⏺ Start recording. The first time, allow microphone access.
  3. Click ⏹ Stop recording. The recording lands on an audio track at the playhead, ready to edit.
Clean it up. Select the recording → Audio tab → Warm voice EQ, Noise & hum reduction and Voice enhance, then Remove silences.

Echo cancellation and noise suppression are on while recording. Export audio only as MP3 or WAV from the Export dialog.

The AI assistant

Describe what you want in plain words, in any language, and the assistant edits your project. You see every change and can undo it in one step.

What it can do

Create

Titles, lower thirds, intros, shapes, color backgrounds and custom SVG stickers and icons.

Animate

Keyframes, in/out animations, motion presets, zoom & pan, splitting illustrations into animated parts.

Edit

Split, trim, move, delete, speed, transitions, effects, effect layers, looks, aspect ratio, markers.

Audio

Remove silences, auto-duck music, normalize, beats to markers, scene detection.

Using it

  • Type in the box and press Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line). Or click a quick prompt: Intro, Lower third, Color grade, Kinetic text, Subscribe button, Make it a Reel, Quick edit, Music.
  • Send frame lets the AI see the current preview frame, for color and composition.
  • Fast / Balanced / Thorough sets how hard the model thinks.
  • Select clips first to say “this clip”. The assistant knows your tracks, clips, media and presets.
  • Each answer is one undo step: Ctrl/⌘+Z takes back everything it did. New chat starts over.

Prompts to try

  • “Make a cinematic 6-second intro titled ‘My Channel’ with animated shapes.”
  • “Add a lower third for ‘Alex Morgan — Director’ at the playhead.”
  • “Make it a vertical Reel and fill the frame with every clip.”
  • “Remove the silences and add short fades between the clips.”
  • “Zoom into the person's face at 3 seconds.”
  • “Put a red subscribe button with a bell in the bottom-right corner for 4 seconds.”
No AI connected? The assistant needs an AI service. See Connect an AI. Every other tool works without one.

AI

Subtitles

Turn speech into timed captions, styled like any text, and export them as an SRT file.

Two ways

Paste the script (AI)

Select the clip with speech, paste what's said in the Auto subtitles box (AI tab) and click Sync script to speech. The app finds where speech happens and the AI splits your text into short captions timed to it.

Automatic transcription

With Whisper installed (pip install openai-whisper), choose the language and click Transcribe & create captions. It runs locally.

  • Captions go on a Captions track. Edit their text, timing and style like any text clip, or save a style as a preset and apply it to all of them.
  • Export SRT saves a subtitle file for YouTube and other players.
Finish

My presets

Set up one clip the way you like, save it, and apply it to any other clip, in any project.

  1. Select the clip and click Save as preset at the top of the Inspector.
  2. Name it and tick what to include. The parts you changed are ticked for you: color, effects, animation, size & position, crop, mask, audio, speed, text style.
  3. To apply, select one or more clips and choose it from ★ My presets. Only the saved parts change.

The ⋯ button manages presets: rename, delete, export one or all to a .nomadpreset.json file to share, and import presets from others. The AI assistant can use your presets too: “apply my teal look to all clips”.

Why it's fast

NomadMovie doesn't play the video back to record it. It decodes the source clips frame by frame, renders each frame on the GPU, encodes it with your computer's hardware video encoder, and mixes the audio separately. An 8-second 1080p video with music takes about 3 seconds on a 2019 Intel laptop. If hardware encoding isn't available, it falls back to real-time recording.

Where it's saved

Save to shows where the file goes: your Movies folder (Videos on Windows) by default. Click Change… to pick another folder or name. When it's done, click Show in Finder (Show in folder on Windows) in the notice.

Finish

Projects and saving

  • Autosave. Your project and its media are saved automatically inside the app as you work, and come back when you reopen it.
  • Save (top bar) downloads a .nomad.json project file: a backup of the edit you can open later with Open.
  • A project file holds the edit, not the media. When you open it on a computer that doesn't have the media yet, the app tells you how many clips are missing so you can re-import the files.
  • New starts an empty project and clears the saved media.

Help

Privacy and security

Your data

  • Your media and projects stay on your computer. The app's engine doesn't listen on the network at all.
  • When you use the AI assistant, a description of your project (and the current frame, if you turn on Send frame) goes to the AI service you chose. Nothing is sent with a local model.
  • AI cut-outs run entirely on your computer. Only the model is downloaded, once, from Hugging Face.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no account.

How the app protects you

  • No network port. The editor window talks to its engine through the app itself, over a private channel protected by a random key. No website or other program can reach it.
  • Safe import. Files are read as local media only. Playlists or file lists that point to other files or web addresses are refused.
  • No code in files. Lottie animations play without expressions, and SVG graphics are drawn as images, so scripts inside them never run.
  • Keys stay put. API keys are saved in a file only your user account can read, and are only sent to the service they belong to.
  • Locked-down window. The editor window is sandboxed with no system access, shows only the editor, and opens other links in your browser.
  • Up to date. The app is built on a supported Electron/Chromium version, and dependencies are watched by Dependabot.

Found a security problem? Please report it privately through GitHub security advisories instead of a public issue.

Troubleshooting and FAQ

macOS says the app “can't be opened” or is “damaged”

Right-click NomadMovie in Applications → Open → Open. If that doesn't show an Open button: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. This is needed once because the app isn't distributed through the App Store.

Windows SmartScreen blocks the installer

Click More info → Run anyway, or use the portable .zip.

The AI pill in the top bar is grey

No AI is connected yet. Click it (or ⚙ Settings) and follow Connect an AI. For the Claude subscription, click Sign in with Claude and finish the sign-in in your browser.

“Whisper” is grey

Automatic transcription is optional and needs pip install openai-whisper. Without it, paste the script and use Sync script to speech.

A file won't import

Almost every format is converted automatically. If one still fails, the message says why; the file may be damaged or protected (DRM). Converting it to MP4 elsewhere and importing that always works.

AI cut-out is slow

It's fastest on a GPU with WebGPU. On older computers, track every 3rd or 5th frame instead of every frame. The outline is interpolated in between.

Playback stutters

Lower the preview quality (menu next to the volume in the preview). This doesn't affect the export.

How do I update?

NomadMovie checks for new versions by itself. With the Windows installer or the Linux AppImage, it downloads the update and shows Restart to update. On macOS (and with the zips or the .deb) it shows a Download button that brings you to this site; install the new version over the old one. Your projects stay.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything except the online AI services works offline, including fonts and export. The AI cut-out model needs internet once to download.

Is it really free?

Yes: free and open source under the MIT license, with no watermark. AI services you connect may cost money; a local model or your existing Claude plan doesn't add anything.

Something else?

Open an issue on GitHub with what you did, what happened and your system.

Build from source and contribute

You need Node.js 22 or newer.

git clone https://github.com/faridmasjedi/NomadMovie.git
cd NomadMovie
npm install

npm run dev        # run in the browser at http://localhost:5173, with hot reload
npm run app        # build and open the desktop app
npm run dist       # build an installer for your system into release/

How it's built

React, TypeScript and Vite for the interface; a Canvas and WebGL compositor for rendering; Web Audio for sound; WebCodecs for fast export; Electron for the desktop app; and an engine with a bundled ffmpeg for conversion, export and AI calls. In the desktop app the window reaches the engine through Electron and a private socket, so nothing listens on the network.
